How it works

Your executives keep doing podcasts, webinars and talks. We turn each one into a week of LinkedIn posts.

  1. We find the recordingWe pick up your published episodes and webinars, or you send a file or a link. Nobody on your side records anything new.
  2. We cut and writeThe moments worth posting, chosen and checked by a person, captioned for sound-off, each with its LinkedIn post. Grouped by your monthly themes.
  3. You postEverything arrives within 24 hours: the clips, the posts, the captions and covers. Ask for any change and we re-cut it.

What every clip comes with

Ready to post the moment it arrives. Professional, accurate and consistent with your brand.

A vertical clip, cut by a person

The moment an executive would want to be known for, 35 seconds to 3 minutes long: it starts where the idea starts and ends when they've landed the point. Never cut off to hit a time limit.

A LinkedIn post

A hook, three takeaways in the speaker's own words and a closing question that invites comments. Nothing invented.

A Shorts & Reels caption

A title, a description and a handful of fitting hashtags for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Executive captions

Calm two-line captions in sentence case, synced to the voice. No bouncing, all-caps creator styling.

A steady, brand-safe edit

The speaker full-screen with a still camera, a short topic header, and a name and title only when we've confirmed who's speaking.

A captions file and a cover

An .srt file for native LinkedIn captions and a cover image for every clip. No watermark: everything is yours.

Questions

Do we need to record anything new?

No. We work from what your executives already did: your podcast, webinars, panels, conference talks and guest appearances. Camera shots and slides alike.

How long is each clip?

As long as the point takes: 35 seconds to 3 minutes. Each clip opens on the question, claim or story setup and ends on the sentence where the speaker lands it, never mid-thought.

Who picks the moments?

A person, every time. We look for what an executive would want to be known for, check every cut, and never add words they didn't say.

What's in the free sample?

3 finished clips from one recording, each with its LinkedIn post, Shorts & Reels caption, captions file and cover. On a private page, yours to keep.

Who owns the clips?

You do. No watermark, and they're yours to post anywhere.

What's the guarantee?

If your first 12 clips don't beat your last month of LinkedIn posts on impressions, your next month is free. Compare them in your own LinkedIn analytics.

Do you post for us?

You post from your own profiles, so the reach stays yours. Everything arrives ready to paste: the clip, the post, the captions file and the cover.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month after a 2-week trial.
